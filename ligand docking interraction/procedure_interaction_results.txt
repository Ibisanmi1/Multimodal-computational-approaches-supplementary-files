Procedure for Obtaining Protein–Ligand Interaction Results

Protein–ligand interaction results were obtained from the top-ranked docking poses for each compound across Groups A–D. For each docked complex, the protein–ligand PDB structure was parsed to extract protein and ligand atom coordinates. Interactions were identified using distance-based criteria: hydrogen bonds were assigned where donor–acceptor distances were ≤3.5 Å (or ≤2.5 Å for hydrogen-to-acceptor contacts), hydrophobic contacts where carbon–carbon distances were ≤4.5 Å, electrostatic or salt-bridge interactions where charged residue–ligand distances were ≤4.0 Å, and pi-stacking interactions where aromatic ring distances were ≤5.0 Å. Each contact was classified by residue identity, ligand atom or functional group, and interaction type (hydrogen bond, electrostatic, pi-cation, pi-stacking, hydrophobic, halogen bond, or related types).

The automated interaction tables were then curated and annotated to produce structured CSV files for each compound. Each record included the protein residue, position, interaction type, exact ligand atom or group, ligand structural part, and a concise interaction description. Additional structural moieties visible in the 2D interaction diagrams but not forming direct bonds were recorded separately. The resulting interaction datasets were organised by group (groupA, groupB, groupc, groupd) and used for scaffold-level aggregation. Residue-contact events were tabulated by interaction class to identify dominant amino-acid hotspots and scaffold-dependent binding behaviour across the evaluated compounds.
